## What the Evaluation Found

The sharpest finding across all 12 hater personas: **"Who is running this, and why should I trust them with my child?"** Founder anonymity was the most consistent vulnerability — without a named, credible operator, both parents and sponsors lack a reason to commit.

The evaluation also surfaced:

- **Missing ticket price** — the entire revenue model is built on ticket sales but the price is never stated
- **Break-even ≠ business** — the $5K–$7.5K revenue target covers hard costs but not founder labor; the model earns zero for the operator at Event 1
- **Student presenter cold-start** — the most differentiated moment (kids on stage) has no defined recruitment system
- **Premium is asserted, not demonstrated** — "premium venue" is doing significant work in the pitch but has no concrete visual referent

**Sharpest strength:** "Kids presenting their real work to a real audience" is genuinely differentiated from every identified competitor — Maker Faire, FIRST Robotics, TEDx, and science festivals all lack this combination of curation + student voice + premium positioning.

**Best opportunity:** Content capture from Event 1 (video of student talks + expert presentations) creates compounding marketing assets with viral potential at near-zero marginal cost.
