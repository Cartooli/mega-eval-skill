# Evaluation Brief

## Subject
Future Builders Live — a ticketed family live event series

## Core Proposition
A premium, sponsor-supported live event series where kids hear inspiring short talks, see hands-on technology demos, and present their own work on stage—positioned as a TED-style family experience built around curiosity, confidence, and real-world skills, funded by ticket sales, local sponsors, and community partners.

## Key Claims & Features
- 2.5–3 hour Saturday events in premium rented venues (100–120 person capacity)
- Format: 3 adult talks + 4–6 student show-and-tell presentations + 1 featured demo/keynote + hands-on stations + networking + sponsor tables
- Revenue streams: family tickets, local sponsors, vendor/demo tables, post-event workshops, memberships
- 120-person event projects $5,000–$7,500+ revenue (stated as sufficient to cover all expenses)
- Parents remain on-site throughout (family event, not drop-off childcare)
- Topics: AI, robotics, coding, design, science, entrepreneurship
- First event theme: "Kids, AI, Robots & Big Ideas"
- Goal of first event: break even, prove demand, capture testimonials, recruit recurring sponsors

## Target Audience
- **Primary attendees:** Families with school-age children (age range unspecified in pitch)
- **Community participants:** Local educators, founders, engineers, artists, scientists, makers
- **Sponsors:** Education, technology, healthcare, finance, and community businesses
- **Presenters:** Local adult experts (paid) + student show-and-tell participants
