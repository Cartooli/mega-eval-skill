# Synthesis: Critical Fixes, Design Issues & Next Steps

## Critical Fixes Needed

### Fix 1: Clarify the product narrative
- **What:** The pitch overstates certainty relative to the thin evidence gathered in early analysis.
- **Why it matters:** Overclaiming weakens trust and makes the product look less rigorous than the workflow promises.
- **Flagged by:** Phase 1A critical feedback and Phase 1B limited-data market analysis.
- **Suggested approach:** Reframe claims around hypotheses, evidence, and confidence levels.

## Design Inconsistencies to Resolve
The current messaging implies a polished public product experience, but the available material is still closer to an internal workflow than a finished surface.

## Proposed Next Steps (Non-Breaking Changes)
Move from stronger claims to better evidence and clearer product framing.

### Quick Wins (days)
Rewrite the opening narrative so it reflects the actual maturity of the product.

### Medium-Term (weeks)
Collect examples, public proof points, and a cleaner explanation of how the workflow differs from a one-shot prompt.

### Strategic (months)
Build a stronger body of usage evidence before making broad market claims.

## Unresolved Tensions
The strengths analysis says the workflow structure is the moat, while the hater-style critique says it still looks easy to copy. Both can be true: the implementation is simple, but the value only holds if the output quality keeps proving the methodology matters.
